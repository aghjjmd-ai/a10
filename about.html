About Crumbly

We are here for the moment a recipe says “1 cup” and you own a scale.

Crumbly is an independent home-baking site. We focus on the practical bits that decide whether a bake works: measuring, pans and oven temperature.

Our converters answer the quick questions; our guides explain the reasons behind them so you can adapt any recipe with confidence.

Tested

Conversions are checked against weighed measurements in a home kitchen.

Independent

We do not sell ingredients or equipment, and nobody pays to be mentioned.

Reviewed

Every guide is read by a second editor before it is published.
